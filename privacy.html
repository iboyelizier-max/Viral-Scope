Privacy Policy — ViralScope

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ViralScope handles information in connection with its TikTok Account Analyzer features and integrations. ViralScope is an independent application and is not owned, operated, affiliated with, or endorsed by TikTok.

1. Overview

ViralScope is a web-based tool designed to help users view and analyze information associated with TikTok accounts that they choose to connect. The application may use TikTok for Developers and TikTok Login Kit to provide account connection and related features.

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled in connection with ViralScope's website and its TikTok integration. By using features that connect a TikTok account, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

Independent service: ViralScope is an independent application. It is not owned, operated, sponsored, affiliated with, or officially supported or endorsed by TikTok.

2. Information We May Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user and the capabilities available through the TikTok integration, ViralScope may access certain TikTok information made available through the requested scopes, including:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, such as information made available through the user's authorized TikTok profile.
  • Profile statistics, which may include follower, following, and likes information when made available through the authorized integration.
  • Video information available through the video.list scope, subject to the permissions and data made available by TikTok.

The exact information available to ViralScope can depend on the permissions granted by the user, TikTok's platform capabilities, and applicable restrictions or changes to the TikTok API.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through an authorized TikTok connection may be used only as needed to provide the features requested by the user, including:

  • Displaying information about a connected TikTok account.
  • Analyzing account and content performance.
  • Generating analytical insights based on available account or video information.
  • Creating recommendations, scores, categories, indicators, or other analytical outputs developed by ViralScope.
  • Supporting the operation of the requested ViralScope features and the TikTok account connection.
Analytical results are not official TikTok metrics. Any score, recommendation, category, rating, indicator, or other analytical result produced by ViralScope is an analysis generated by ViralScope based on information available to the application. It should not be interpreted as an official metric, ranking, recommendation, or assessment provided by TikTok.

4. TikTok Login and Account Credentials

ViralScope may use TikTok Login Kit to allow a user to authorize a connection between ViralScope and the user's TikTok account.

ViralScope does not ask users to provide or enter their TikTok password to ViralScope. Authentication and authorization are handled through the mechanisms provided by TikTok.

Where access tokens are required to support an authorized integration, they will be handled through appropriate security measures and, when server-side handling is required, through a secure server-side mechanism. The specific storage and processing architecture may depend on the final implementation of the ViralScope integration. This policy does not state that an access token is stored when such storage has not been implemented.

5. Data Sharing and Service Providers

ViralScope does not sell users' TikTok data.

Information accessed through TikTok is intended to be used to provide the ViralScope features requested by the user. ViralScope may rely on hosting, infrastructure, security, storage, networking, or other service providers that are reasonably necessary to operate the website and its integrations. Such providers may process information only as needed to provide or support the relevant services, subject to applicable contractual, technical, and legal requirements.

ViralScope does not intend to use TikTok information for unrelated purposes that are inconsistent with the features requested by the user.

6. Data Security

ViralScope takes reasonable measures appropriate to the application's implementation to protect information handled through its services. Security measures may include access controls, secure communications, and appropriate handling of authorization credentials or tokens where applicable.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their own devices and TikTok account credentials and use TikTok's official authentication mechanisms when connecting an account.

7. Your Choices and TikTok Authorization

Connecting a TikTok account is optional. A user may choose not to connect a TikTok account and may continue to use any ViralScope features that are available without such authorization.

A user may also revoke or withdraw authorization through mechanisms made available by TikTok. After authorization is revoked, ViralScope may no longer be able to access information that depends on that authorization.

Revocation of authorization does not necessarily constitute a request to delete information that may already have been handled by a service. For deletion requests, please see the section below.

8. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their use of ViralScope where applicable.

To submit a deletion request, users should contact the service operator using the contact information currently available on the ViralScope website. The request should provide enough information for the operator to identify the relevant ViralScope account, connection, or data without unnecessarily including passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

The service operator will review the request and determine the appropriate action based on the information involved, the current ViralScope implementation, applicable legal obligations, and any information that must be retained for legitimate security, legal, or operational purposes.

If a user wants to stop future access to their TikTok account, they should also revoke the relevant authorization through the mechanisms provided by TikTok.

Important: Because ViralScope's TikTok integration may be under active development, this policy does not claim that a particular category of TikTok data is currently stored or that a particular automated deletion mechanism is already implemented. Deletion requests will be handled according to the applicable data and the implementation in effect at the time of the request.

TikTok is a third-party service. Its privacy practices, terms, data handling, and platform rules are governed by TikTok's own policies and terms. Users should review the information provided by TikTok when using TikTok or authorizing a connection to ViralScope.

ViralScope does not control TikTok's services or policies and does not represent itself as TikTok or as an official TikTok application.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to ViralScope in a manner that is not permitted, they may contact the service operator using the contact information available on the ViralScope website so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be considered.

11. Data Retention

ViralScope aims to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain the service, address security or technical issues, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

The specific retention period for any category of information depends on the final technical implementation and how the information is used. This policy does not claim that a particular category of TikTok information is permanently stored.
